CS2680 Modern AI Systems: Agents and System Optimizations Fall 2026 · Mon & Wed 11:15am–12:30pm · SEC LL2.221

This course follows one AI system from the agent loop down to the GPU. Students start by using agent frameworks, then build their own, measure performance, and optimize on top of rental APIs. They then deploy open-source models themselves and work through the underlying systems layers: GPU architecture and Triton kernels, attention optimization, batching and scheduling, KV-cache management, routing, quantization, and speculative decoding. Throughout, the emphasis is on defining success metrics, accounting for every token and second, and measuring improvements at each layer.
Prerequisites: at least one of CS61, CS1610, or CS2620; proficiency in Python and PyTorch.

CS2640 Modern Computer Storage Systems Spring 2026 · Mon & Wed 11:15am–12:30pm · SEC LL2.223

A comprehensive examination of both the hardware and software components of modern computer storage systems, covering the layered architecture from individual disks and SSDs to block storage, file systems, and distributed storage systems, with an emphasis on efficiency, performance, and reliability. Topics include caching, RAID and data protection, machine learning for storage, and storage for machine learning workloads. The first half is lecture-based; the second half features student paper presentations, discussions, and guest lectures from industry experts.
Prerequisites: at least one of CS61, CS1610, or CS2620.
